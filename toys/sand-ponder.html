This is the whole visual: a JSON header listing its knobs, then the shader. It's a falling-sand game run on the graphics card. The screen is a grid of grains; each step it is cut into 2x2 blocks, and in each block a grain falls if the cell below is free, or slips off a pile sideways. The blocks shift by one cell every step so sand flows. Tilting the phone turns gravity: each step picks the down or sideways direction at random, weighted by the tilt, so tipped sand slides at an angle. The Pondering look, after the piece "continuous pondering", draws each grain as an overlapping disc of fine curled lines. On the party screen, House Party would turn the same knobs from the music instead of your fingers.
