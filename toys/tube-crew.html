This is the whole visual: a JSON header listing its knobs, then the shader. The page simulates every dancer as a chain of fabric tube 120 times a second: air pressure pulls each section straight, gravity folds it over, gusts and turbulence make the arms flail, and the tassels flutter. The air comes on the beat (Groove puffs every beat, Wave rolls across the crowd once a bar, Flop collapses half of them and whips them back up, Chaos, and Sync where they all move as one), plus whatever you add by tapping, shaking the phone, a MIDI keyboard or the microphone. The chains are packed into a small image, one row per dancer, and the shader traces each tube exactly as a string of capsules with a face, tassels and a blower box, lit with soft shadows on the ground. Cameras cut on the bar in Auto. Every 16 bars a flying saucer may drift over and pull them skyward with its beam. On the party screen, House Party would turn the same knobs from the music instead of your fingers.
