This is the whole visual: a JSON header listing its knobs and its two passes, then the shader. Pass 1 draws every filament into a screen-sized glow buffer that fades a little each frame, so moving fingers leave plasma trails. Each bolt is a main filament, a thinner twin and a fork that jumps on every strike; the Plasma or lightning knob blends a slow smooth wander with straight-line zigzags. Pass 2 paints the sky in the current chord's colour and the electricity in its complement. On the party screen, House Party would turn the same knobs from the music instead of your fingers.
