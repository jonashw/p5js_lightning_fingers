This is the whole visual: a JSON header listing its knobs, then the shader. A faithful port of Jon's p5.js toy. Each finger gets a ten-step bolt from the centre, redrawn with new jitter every frame, over a background whose hue walks all the way round in about half a minute. The page plays one more note of a C13 chord for each finger. Let go and a small spark flies home; tap the centre dot to freeze the sparks where they are. On the party screen, House Party would turn the same knobs from the music instead of your fingers.
